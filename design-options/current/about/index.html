About us

The smart voice for smart technology.

More than one out of four Americans hear Liz’s voice on a daily basis. You hear that distinctive voice in commercials, corporate films, e-learning, web, and multimedia.

For more than twenty five years, Liz Raphael Helgesen has been the smart voice for smart technology — “the voice” that millions of people throughout the United States and the world hear every day when they use mobile phones, engage their interactive media for banking and financial needs, are placed on hold when calling businesses, and learn of public health outbreaks.

She is the “voice on the move,” guiding people who ride public transit and use their GPS or auto NAV systems to reach their destinations.

Now, with the formal introduction of multi-language voiceover talent, Passion Fruit is giving you even more passion along with the same unparalleled service experience you’ve had for years.

A human touch.

Liz and Passion Fruit’s purpose is to bring a human touch to our world’s mobile, automated society and help people get what they need to live better lives. She is an expert communicator; a master at the art of human expression using the spoken word.

By delivering outstanding customer service and a warm, sincere voice, Liz consistently reaches people in a fast-paced, faceless, instant communication society. Her voice is insured by Lloyd’s of London.

In 1996, Liz formed Passion Fruit Voiceovers, Inc. to focus solely on her craft, after a corporate career as Vice President of Human Resources and years of voice imaging for international brands that began in 1985.
