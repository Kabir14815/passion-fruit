2015

Chabad of Cobb Community Service Award

Voice of Service for Chabad, MARTA. By Cady Schulman, Jewish Times, March 20, 2015.

“For Liz Helgesen, volunteering several days a week at Chabad of Cobb is just one way she can give back to the synagogue she says has helped her grow as a Jewish woman. To honor Helgesen for her hours of service, synagogue officials selected her as this year’s recipient of the Chabad of Cobb Service Award.”

2014

Women’s Spa for the Soul — keynote

At the 2014 Women’s Spa for the Soul and 2013 Women of Weber, Liz presented “Bringing Heaven to the Mundane,” an inspiring journey of recovery and self-discovery. She shared an uplifting talk about fulfilling her life’s purpose of bringing Heaven down to earth through elevating life’s most mundane tasks.

AIB-TV

Atlanta Interfaith Broadcasting

As a recovery woman of 27 years, Liz’s passion and calling is to serve communities with a universal message of hope and healing. Through keynote addresses, TV appearances, and Wisdom Meetings, she moves audiences to action. “Addiction is more than just to substances. We are all addicted to attitudes, beliefs, and behaviors that keep us from living our full lives, from realizing our full potential and true purpose in this world.”

With gratitude, Liz guest hosted two episodes of AIB-TV’s The Circle, a faith-related talk show hosted by accomplished Atlanta women.

ServiceSpace.org

Liz joined the Service Team at ServiceSpace.org. She records an audio version of their Wisdom Readings — passages sent electronically and heard by over 2.2 million people throughout the world each week. “An experiment in the joy of giving,” ServiceSpace is a non-profit run entirely by volunteers.
