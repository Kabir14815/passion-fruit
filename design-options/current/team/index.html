Setting the tone for business communications since the emergence of interactive voice technology, Liz is the nation’s premier talent for global interactive voice media. Twenty five years of voice imaging for international brands.

Liz embarked on a stellar career early in 1985 using her distinctive voice and expressing her passion to communicate in the emerging Interactive Voice Response business. As Vice President of Human Resources in the corporate world, Liz excelled as a public speaker, lecturing on morale building, inspiring hundreds of others and creating healthy corporate cultures. At the same time, she continued to perform voiceovers for commercials, corporate video narration, and IVR systems for companies across the country. In 1996, Liz formed Passion Fruit Voiceovers, Inc. to focus solely on her craft.

Liz and Passion Fruit’s purpose is to bring a human touch to our world’s mobile, automated society and help people get what they need to live better lives. She is an expert communicator; a master at the art of human expression using the spoken word. She has an innate ability to understand and connect with people. Liz’s voice is insured by Lloyd’s of London.
